Services

AI that does a real job in your business

Implementation, automation, and a regular advisory day if you want a steady hand. Work starts from A$600 per day.

AI implementation

I put AI to work in the parts of your business that already take time. That can include voice AI for phone reception and outbound calling, help on a Shopify store, or drafting and routing everyday work on your website.

We start with one job, test it on real work, then go live. I set the operating rules so each task has a clear owner, and nothing is published or paid for without your approval.

Automation

The same details should not be typed into three different systems. I connect the tools you already use — including Shopify, Xero, Meta and Google Ads, invoicing and websites — so work can move from one step to the next.

The aim is practical: fewer repeat jobs, a clearer picture of what is going on, and a person still in charge of money and anything public.

Fractional AI advisory

A regular day or two to help you decide what is worth automating, set the rules, and keep the systems honest. This is hands-on work, not a report you are left to implement alone.

Engagements are month to month. You can scale the days up or down as the work changes.

How an engagement works

Clear scope, then we build

  • We talk through the work that eats your week and agree one useful starting job.
  • I tell you what I will set up, what I need from you, and the day rate — from A$600 per day (AUD).
  • I build and test before anything goes live.
  • You approve anything that spends money or is published.
  • We review, then decide whether to keep going month to month.

What I work with

In my own online businesses I direct specialised AI agents that run voice AI on live telephone lines for reception and outbound calling, Shopify stores, Xero, Meta and Google Ads, invoicing and websites. That is the same kind of practical work I can set up for you.
