I set up AI and automation for businesses, from the Gold Coast. I still do the work myself.

From running a fleet to running AI systems

I founded Auswide Car Transport Pty Ltd, which traded as Chase Limousines from 2017, and sold the business in 2022. It was a multi-brand passenger transport group with about 100 staff and an 87-vehicle fleet.

I ran the operation day to day: dispatch, fleet, rostering, training, and the written procedures that kept the work consistent.

I now run a portfolio of online businesses through a production team of more than 20 specialised AI agents. I set the operating rules: one owner per task, brands kept separate, approval required before spending money or publishing, and every project reporting back to one manager.

The agents run voice AI on live telephone lines for reception and outbound calling, Shopify stores, Xero, Meta and Google Ads, invoicing and websites. I direct the work and check it before it goes out.
